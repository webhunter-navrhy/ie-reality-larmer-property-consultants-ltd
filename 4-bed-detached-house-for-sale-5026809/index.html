Description

Larmer Property are delighted to bring to the market Millbrook House, Clones, Co. Monaghan, H23 HT61.

This impressive period residence sits on a substantial, elevated site, offering a rare opportunity to acquire a property of true character and scale. Commanding sweeping views of Clones town and the historic church, the home boasts generous accommodation throughout that has been thoughtfully refurbished and modernised by the current owners. The result is a seamless blend of original charm and contemporary living, creating a truly exceptional family home in a unique and picturesque setting.

Perched in a spectacular elevated position, this residence is defined by its sweeping, manicured gardens that wrap around the front, side, and rear. The exterior is perfectly framed by traditional wrought iron fencing to the front and sides, transitioning to a classic ranch-style fence towards the rear, ensuring both privacy and aesthetic appeal.

This spacious home beautifully balances character and functionality, featuring four generous bedrooms, including a convenient ground-floor bedroom with en-suite, and three spacious reception rooms, two of which are adorned with stunning bay windows that flood the interiors with natural light, making this an ideal residence for comfortable family living. The windows throughout are uPVC doubled glazed, with all front windows have been replaced in August 2026, as well as the beautiful new front door.

Accommodation in brief at ground floor : Entrance Hall, Sitting Room, Lounge, Living / Dining Room, bedroom 1 (En-Suite).

First floor accommodation : 3 No. Double bedroom, and the family bathroom.
